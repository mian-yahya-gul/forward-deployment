# 1. Purpose

The Careers section introduces how DeosAI Labs builds its team, communicates its culture, and attracts exceptional talent.

Rather than functioning solely as a job board, the Careers experience should explain the company's mission, engineering philosophy, learning culture, and long-term vision.

The objective is to help candidates determine whether DeosAI Labs is the right place for them before applying.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The Careers section should:

• Attract exceptional talent.

• Communicate company culture.

• Explain engineering values.

• Reduce mismatched applications.

• Strengthen employer branding.

• Support long-term hiring.

• Showcase growth opportunities.
