# 1. Purpose

The About page explains who DeosAI Labs is, why the company exists, what it believes, and how it approaches AI transformation.

Its objective is not to promote services, but to establish trust, communicate the company's philosophy, and help visitors understand the people, principles, and long-term vision behind the organization.

The About page should reinforce that DeosAI Labs is committed to practical, responsible, and measurable AI transformation.

---

# 2. Business Objectives

The About page should:

• Build trust and credibility.

• Explain the company's purpose.

• Communicate long-term vision.

• Differentiate DeosAI Labs from traditional software agencies.

• Highlight the company's operating principles.

• Introduce leadership and culture.

• Encourage visitors to continue exploring the website or initiate contact.

---

# 3. Target Audience

Primary Audience

• Business Leaders

• Enterprise Decision-Makers

• Potential Clients

• Strategic Partners

Secondary Audience

• Job Candidates

• Students

• Researchers

• Investors (future)

• Community Members

The page should speak to organizations looking for a long-term AI transformation partner.

---

# 4. User Psychology

Visitors are asking:

• Who are these people?

• What do they believe?

• Why should we trust them?

• How do they think about AI?

• Will they be a reliable long-term partner?

The page should answer these questions with clarity and authenticity.

---

# 5. Core Narrative

The About page should tell a coherent story:

Why DeosAI Labs was founded.

↓

The challenges organizations face when adopting AI.

↓

The company's belief that successful AI transformation starts with understanding the business.

↓

The principles that guide every engagement.

↓

The long-term vision for helping organizations use AI responsibly and effectively.

Avoid focusing on company milestones or awards unless they add meaningful context.

---
