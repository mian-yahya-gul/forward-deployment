Working With DeosAI Labs

DeosAI Labs is a forward deployment agency. We don't sell a platform and we don't sell a strategy deck — we embed with your team, build a Digital FTE inside a real workflow, and stay accountable for the outcome.

What an engagement actually looks like​

We follow the five-step model described in The Forward Deployment Model: discovery inside your actual workflow, a scoped design with a measurable before/after, a build against your real systems, a supervised deployment with human checkpoints, and an optimization cycle once it's live. Most first engagements are scoped around a single workflow — the kind described in your industry chapter — not a company-wide transformation program.

How our services map to what you just read​

If a workflow in this book matched something you're living with, here's roughly where it fits into what we offer:

  • AI Strategy & Opportunity Discovery — if you're not yet sure which workflow to start with, this is where we help you find and prioritize it.
  • Intelligent Business Workflows — the core offering behind most of the examples in this book: a Digital FTE embedded in a specific process.
  • Enterprise Knowledge Systems — for organizations whose bottleneck is getting the right information to the right person, not process automation itself.
  • AI Assistants & Operational Copilots — for workflows where the right answer is augmenting a person's judgment rather than automating a step end-to-end.
  • AI Platform Integration — connecting a Digital FTE to the systems of record your team already runs, without replacing them.
  • Continuous Optimization — once a system is live, the ongoing work of tuning it against real outcomes.

Start with a conversation, not a proposal​

The next step isn't a contract — it's a discovery call where we talk through your actual workflow, not a generic version of your industry. Bring the process that's been on your mind since you started this chapter.
